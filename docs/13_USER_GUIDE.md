# 13 — User / Administrator Guide

How to operate an All Elite Cloud workspace. (Screenshots to be added.)

## Getting started

1. Go to `/signup`, enter your organization name, your email, and a password —
   you become the **owner** and are signed in.
2. Sign in later at `/login` (enter your organization, email, password). Forgot
   it? Use **Forgot your password?** → check your email for a reset link.
3. The dashboard (`/app`) is your workspace. Press **Ctrl/Cmd+K** anytime to
   search records or run a quick action.

## The dashboard

- **Command Center / panels** — each business area is a panel. A jump-nav lets
  you move between them.
- **🔔 Notifications** (top right) — invoice paid, proposal accepted, ticket
  opened, website published, teammate added, and more. Click one to open it;
  "Mark all read" clears them.
- **Recent activity** — the latest things that happened across your workspace.

## Core workflow (the customer lifecycle)

1. **Clients** — add the people/companies you work with. Click a client to see
   their **Journey** (every invoice, ticket, proposal, and note in one
   timeline).
2. **Leads (Sales pipeline)** — track prospects new → won. Click a lead's name
   for its journey.
3. **Proposals** — send quotes; accepted/declined proposals appear on the
   client's journey and notify your team.
4. **Projects** — the work you deliver.
5. **Invoices** — bill a client; numbered automatically.
6. **Support tickets** — track requests.
7. **Reviews** — record reputation.

## Marketing & automation

- **Forms** — create a public form (Contact / Quote / Intake), share its link
  or embed it. Submissions are captured and can auto-create a lead.
- **Autoresponders** — build an email sequence with timed steps; enroll people
  manually or automatically when a new lead arrives. `{{name}}` personalizes.
- **Marketing campaigns** — plan and track campaigns.
- **Email** — send email and view your outbox (owner/admin).

## Content & knowledge

- **AI Website Builder** — describe a business and generate a site; publish it
  live on a verified custom domain.
- **Files** — upload and manage documents (10 MB each; storage quota per plan).
- **AI Knowledge Base** — create a collection, add documents, then ask
  questions — answers cite the source documents.
- **Calendar** — track appointments, deadlines, and events.

## Team & account

- **Team** (owner/admin) — invite members with roles (owner/admin/member);
  seats are limited by your plan.
- **Account** — change your password.
- **Plan** — view/change your subscription (Stripe).
- **Branding / domains** — white-label your workspace and connect a custom
  domain.

## Client portal

Your clients can sign in at `/portal` to see only their own projects, invoices,
tickets, and proposals.

## Tips

- Use **Ctrl/Cmd+K** to jump anywhere or create records fast.
- Connect SMTP + Stripe (owner) so email delivery and billing are live.
